The gate

The drift ends here.

Most men don't fall in a day. They drift — quietly, one small compromise at a time, until the husband, the father and the leader they meant to be is a stranger in his own house.

The Kingdom of Disciplined Men exists to end that drift. This is not soft motivation. It is a forge. We take driven men of faith and rebuild them from the inside out — the way a blade is made, with heat and pressure and a hand that knows what it is doing.

Through a 30-day intensive, private coaching, and a brotherhood walking the same road, Larry Beacham helps you become the man your family is already waiting for.

The code

What every disciplined man lives by.

I

Discipline

Do what must be done on the days you don't feel like it. Discipline is the bridge between the man you are and the man you were called to be.

II

Faith

Lead from conviction, not convenience. A man anchored in faith doesn't bend to every storm — he becomes the shelter his family stands under.

III

Family

Be present in the small moments, not just the big ones. Your wife should feel led. Your children should feel safe because you are home.

IV

Brotherhood

No king reigns alone. Iron sharpens iron, and the men beside you will not let you drift back to who you used to be.

Be watchful, stand firm in the faith, act like men, be strong.
1 Corinthians 16:13

The man at the gate

From champion builder to builder of disciplined men.

For years Larry Beacham coached people to win in business, and earned the name The Champion Builder. But the transformation that ran deepest was never the one in a bank account. It was the one at home.

So he turned the work toward men — their marriages, their fathering, their follow-through — and built the Kingdom around the disciplines that actually hold.

"I don't build better men. I help men become who they were already built to be."
